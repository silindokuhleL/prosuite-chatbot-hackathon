## 💡 Pro Tips for Presentation

1. **Start Simple**: Begin with "What can you help me with?"

2. **Show Data Access**: Ask "Calculate total asset value" to show it reads real data

3. **Demonstrate Charts**: Ask "Show asset breakdown" to display visualizations

4. **Use Navigation**: Say "Navigate to risk module" to show quick navigation

5. **Show Patterns**: Ask "Detect risk patterns" to demonstrate analytics

6. **Cross-Module**: Switch between modules to show context awareness
